## 🎯 Highlights

### 1. [Signal 1 - GSE314195 / GSE314211 / GSE314217 Joint-Cabernet 5hmC + 5mC + transcriptome brain atlas, Mus musculus, score 7, n=479 / 479 / 491, pdat 2026/10/29 = FRESHEST NOVEL accession band of the day]: the bioinformatics pick of the day and the first NOVEL hydroxymethylation-resolved brain atlas to enter the retention window. The stated gap is a measurement gap, and it is unusually clean: existing single-cell brain methylome atlases are built on bisulfite conversion, which CANNOT DISTINGUISH 5-hydroxymethylcytosine from 5-methylcytosine - and that ambiguity is not academic in the brain, because postmitotic neurons accumulate abundant 5hmCG which may act in the OPPOSITE direction to 5mCG, so how CpG modification regulates neuronal gene expression has remained incompletely understood. The methodological contribution is the important part: Joint-Cabernet is a bisulfite-free joint single-cell platform that profiles 5hmC, 5mC and the transcriptome simultaneously in the same nucleus, so hydroxymethylation state and expression are measured on the SAME cell rather than inferred across matched atlases. Applied to adult mouse brain nuclei, it resolves cell-type-specific hydroxymethylation landscapes, with elevated 5hmCG and 5hmCH at transcriptionally active genes and regulatory elements across diverse brain cell types, plus spatial gradients across cortical layers. Why it matters for BB: this is the exact measurement upgrade that converts methylation from a bulk, annotation-level readout into a cell-state readout, and it lands directly on the aging axis the longevity lane keeps circling (the authors frame the atlas explicitly as a framework for brain organization, aging and neurodegenerative disease). The 1,449-sample total across the three accessions is also by far the largest cohort in today's NOVEL set, so it can be ingested once and used across several briefs. It also hands the multiomics service a defensible answer to the recurring client objection that bisulfite-based single-cell methylation data is over-interpreted - the rebuttal is that the chemistry can now be fixed.

### 2. [Signal 2 - GSE329077 EXOSC10-lncRNA-dependent checkpoint in nucleolar proteostasis recovery, Homo sapiens, score 6, n=12, pdat 2026/10/29 = FRESHEST NOVEL accession of the day]: the AI drug discovery pick of the day and the first NOVEL RNA-turnover-controlled aggregate-resolution dataset in the window. The framing is a recovery question rather than a damage question: heat-shock-induced proteotoxic stress forms nucleolar aggresomes, and the mechanisms governing their RESOLUTION during recovery were poorly understood. The authors combine temporal quantitative proteomics, transcriptomics and live imaging to define the dynamics of the detergent-insoluble fraction across heat shock and recovery, and find that nucleolar aggresome recovery follows a multiphasic lag-burst-resolution kinetic program that tightly couples recruitment of protein quality-control factors with remobilization of ribosomal proteins. The named mechanism is the contribution: a stress-induced lncRNA, IGS16, accumulates in the nucleolus where it RESTRAINS chaperone engagement, and clearance of IGS16 by the nucleolar exonuclease EXOSC10 is required to initiate nucleolar remodeling and enable HSP70-dependent restoration of ribosomal protein mobility. Critically, IGS16 degradation occurs INDEPENDENTLY of HSP70 ATPase activity, indicating that RNA turnover PRECEDES and LICENSES chaperone-mediated aggregate resolution - a checkpoint ordering, not a correlation. The work also reveals a previously unknown function for EXOSC10 in stress-induced condensate dynamics control, potentially through lncRNA degradation. Why it matters for BB: this is an unusually clean target-nomination geometry - a small-molecule-able exonuclease (EXOSC10) and an antisense-able lncRNA (IGS16) as two orthogonal intervention points on one checkpoint, in human material, with an explicit ordering claim that says which node to hit first. The lncRNA layer is directly adjacent to the peptide/RNA-modulator lane, and the aggresome-resolution framing connects to the senolytic and proteostasis work in the longevity lane without reusing any of its datasets. For AI-DD clients it is also a worked example of the reasoning layer: temporal quantitative proteomics plus transcriptomics plus live imaging, converging on a single checkpoint.
