Glioblastoma is characterized by diffuse infiltration into surrounding brain, which limits complete surgical resection - the strongest single determinant of patient survival - and the invasion mechanism was incompletely understood. The study integrates single-cell transcriptomics, spatial lipidomics, and functional perturbation across mouse models AND human GBM samples, and identifies a lipid-mediated paracrine axis: invading tumor cells engage and reprogram myeloid cells via CSF1R-PI3K signalling, which induces lipid metabolic reprogramming and secretion of bioactive lysophosphatidylcholines and lysophosphatidic acids; these lipids promote invasion through DISTINCT downstream pathways rather than a shared one. Disruption of the myeloid-GBM axis reduces lipid secretion and suppresses invasion, and downstream LPA-LPAR or YAP/TAZ blockade constrains invasion in a context-dependent manner - so the causal chain is tumour cell -> myeloid cell -> lipid mediator -> invasion, with two named intervention points at different levels of the same circuit. Why it matters for BB: the tumour-cell-intrinsic version of this story has been digested repeatedly (id:139's PD-L1/PDK4-GLS NSCLC metabolic escape, id:138's CAF-EV-ANP32A GSH axis), but here the producer of the pathogenic signal is the MYELOID compartment and the effector class is LIPIDS - a mediator class that no prior entry in the window has nominated as the actionable layer, which is precisely the kind of modality novelty a target-nomination client is buying. ### 2. [Signal 2 - GSE326786 + GSE326776 nail-mesenchyme BMP-rich ligand environment, regeneration versus fibrotic wound healing, Mus musculus, score 7 (n=4) + score 4 (n=98), pdat 2026/10/26]: the fibrosis-lane pick of the day, and the first NOVEL regeneration-versus-fibrosis dataset in the retention window - it inverts the standard fibrosis framing. The adult digit tip is one of the few privileged mammalian tissues that can regenerate; the study asks WHY, and answers with a positive control rather than a pathology atlas: using single-cell spatial transcriptomics plus genetic mouse models, it shows regeneration REQUIRES the nail organ and its associated inductive nail mesenchyme, and that in their absence the digit tip undergoes fibrotic wound healing. The nail organ/mesenchyme orchestrates a highly organized regenerative response in which mesenchymal cells are reprogrammed to a BLASTEMA state and re-epithelialization is enhanced - and it does so by creating a regeneration-specific ligand environment that is ABSENT in the non-regenerative condition and includes multiple BMPs. Deleting Smad4 in all mesenchymal cells, or specifically in the nail mesenchyme, inhibits multiple aspects of the regenerative response including expression of the regeneration-specific ligands. Why it matters for BB: this is a rare fibrosis dataset that supplies the POSITIVE arm of the axis, and the positive arm is what anti-fibrotic briefs actually need - a client does not want to know what fibrotic tissue looks like, they want to know what regeneration looks like so they can try to reproduce it. Because the effector is a locally secreted ligand environment (multiple BMPs) rather than a single receptor, the therapeutic hypothesis is a paracrine-factor delivery brief, which maps onto the peptide lane far more directly than any other dataset in today's scan. ### 3. [Signal 3 - GSE351767 adipose mitochondrial fitness rewires systemic aging via the Serpina3m adipokine, Mus musculus, score 4, n=4, pdat 2026/10/27 = FRESHEST NOVEL accession of the day]: the longevity pick, and a mechanistically unusual one - it moves the control point from the target tissue to an ENDOCRINE OUTPUT. Mitochondrial dysfunction is a hallmark of aging, but whether restoring mitochondrial function in adipose tissue ALONE can delay systemic aging was unknown. Adipose-specific upregulation of the mitochondrial chaperone DsbA-L improves metabolic and cognitive function and extends lifespan; it reprograms adipose gene expression and raises circulating levels of the adipokine Serpina3m. Fat-body-specific knockdown of Spn42De (CG9460), the Drosophila serpin counterpart of Serpina3m, shortens lifespan, while its overexpression extends it; the human ortholog SERPINA3 is enriched in CENTENARIANS. Crucially, DsbA-L couples metabolic efficiency to endocrine signalling rather than acting as a conventional mitochondrial enhancer, and BERBERINE increases DsbA-L expression - so the pathway is pharmacologically tractable with an existing small molecule. Benefits occur in both sexes. Why it matters for BB: this is the first entry in the retention window that treats a mitochondrial chaperone as an UPSTREAM lever whose output is a circulating endocrine factor, which reframes the longevity lane from 'which mitochondrial pathway' to 'which secreted factor' - and Serpina3m/SERPINA3 is a secreted protein, i.e. a direct peptide-adjacent target for the longevity lane, with cross-species validation in fruit fly and a human centenarian enrichment signal that gives the brief immediate translational texture. It also answers a question BB clients actually ask (can one tissue's mitochondrial restoration buy whole-organism benefit?), with a yes and a named mediator.
