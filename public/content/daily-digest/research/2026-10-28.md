## 🎯 Highlights

### 1. [Signal 1 - GSE324612 TREM2+ macrophages of distinct developmental origin in diabetic kidney disease, Mus musculus, score 7, n=4, pdat 2026/10/09]: the highest-scoring NOVEL accession of the day and the first NOVEL renal-fibrosis entry to enter the retention window. The JASN paper (doi: 10.1681/ASN.0000001248) integrates scRNA-seq, spatial transcriptomics of diabetic OVE26 kidneys, and reciprocal bone-marrow transplantation in a high-fat-diet plus streptozotocin model. Global TREM2 deficiency markedly worsened albuminuria, lowered GFR, aggravated glomerular and tubular injury - TREM2 is a protective factor, not a pathogenic one. The spatial component resolves that TREM2 is induced in TWO distinct macrophage populations in two distinct anatomic niches: resident-associated macrophages in the glomerulus and monocyte-derived macrophages in the tubulointerstitium. The chimeras then show the populations are functionally compartmentalized: resident-derived TREM2 protects the glomerulus, donor-derived TREM2 limits tubulointerstitial injury. This is a cell-of-origin-resolved, spatially-localized, functionally-separated protective axis with a directly actionable target (TREM2 agonism, or origin-restricted myeloid engineering). Why it matters for BB: Brown Biotech's fibrosis lane has been built on fibroblast and ECM biology; this is the myeloid-compartment counterpart, and the compartment-specific point is exactly the kind of design constraint a fibrosis brief needs - a systemic TREM2 agonist would not be equivalent to a glomerulus-restricted one. It is also the cleanest example in today's scan of the standard BB methodology template: cell-type state from scRNA-seq, spatial localization from spatial transcriptomics, causal assignment from chimera.
