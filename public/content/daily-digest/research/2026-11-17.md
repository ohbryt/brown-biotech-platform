The clinical framing is the commercially crowded T-cell engager field, and the study enters it from the failure mode rather than from another target. Reported result: single-agent TCE treatment of cancer patients induces T-cell hyporesponsiveness including abolished proliferative and lytic capacity; single-cell RNA-sequencing identified transcriptional features of anergy/quiescence like upregulation of CBLB and BACH2 and suppression of AP-1-dependent activation programmes due to isolated provision of T-cell signal-1; and the engineering answer is bivalent bispecific costimulators (BiCos) which deliver signal-2 via highly efficient bivalent CD28 binding yet maintain strictly target-restricted activity. Three arms make this actionable rather than descriptive. Preclinical analyses document that BiCos potentiate the efficacy of single-agent TCE treatment, with activity exceeding that of univalent knob-into-hole CD28 costimulators, and revert T-cell hyporesponsiveness; in humanised mice, BiCos induced elimination of established tumours in combination with very low doses of TCE; and BiCos reversed molecular hallmark features of quiescence such as high expression of BACH2 and KLF2 in T cells. Why it matters for BB: this reframes the well-known TCE-works-then-stops problem from a toxicity or manufacturing constraint into a mechanistically specified and chemically fixable problem - the missing variable is signal-2 avidity and target restriction, not a new tumour antigen. For a refractory-cancer brief the commercially decisive output is a bispecific-format design brief: bivalent CD28 engagement plus strict tumour-restricted targeting, with the patient-derived single-cell data as the biomarker backbone (CBLB/BACH2/AP-1 programme score as the on-target engagement readout). Why it matters for BB, on three levels. Second, the coverage is deliberately broad across melanoma (A375, SK-MEL5), lung (A549, A549-B, H1299), colorectal (HCT15, RKO, SW620), cervical (HeLa), gastric (HUTU80, KELLY), renal (RD), breast (MCF-7, MDA-MB-231), ovarian (SKOV3) and brain/CNS (LN229, U87) backgrounds, so a signature derived here is line-agnostic rather than tied to one histology - a direct fit for the AI-drug-discovery service's assay-design and target-nomination briefs, and a candidate off-the-shelf response panel for clients who need a stress-response comparator. Third, the 4 h versus 24 h split gives immediate-early and late program layers, which is what makes it useful for distinguishing DNA-damage signalling from downstream consequence. A caution worth stating plainly: cell-line radiation response is not equal to clinical radiosensitivity, and this dataset should be read as a signature and assay-design resource rather than as a clinical predictor.
