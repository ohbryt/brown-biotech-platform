The key methodological claim is that regulatory dynamics are CONTINUOUS and NON-LINEAR, so binary young-versus-old contrasts systematically mis-read them - a direct methodological argument that maps onto BB's own multiomics service, where age-bin design is the most common client-side failure. The authors reason from the observation that pathogen-driven γδ T cell responses, including CMV-driven ones, exhibit greater functional diversity and durability, and use that as the conceptual frame for a multimodal activation strategy. It also demonstrates the multi-signal activation principle in a way a target-nomination brief can reuse directly, and the attenuated-Listeria vector modality gives the AI-DD lane a completely different modality class from anything in the retention window. The dataset also directly answers the refractory-cancer lane's stock question - what does the immune effector look like when the first-line activator has driven it to exhaustion - because ZOL and QUAIL-100 are assayed side by side on the same patient samples.
