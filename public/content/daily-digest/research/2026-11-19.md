The design is a cross-species spatial design rather than a single-arm atlas: spatial transcriptomics on human sarcoid lung samples, with adjacent non-sarcoid tissue and normal controls as the within-patient comparison, anchored to the Tsc1/2-knockout mouse sarcoidosis model to identify genetic contributors to granuloma formation. The mechanism is stated precisely and is the commercially useful part: reperfusion of ischemic myocardium triggers a sterile inflammatory cascade in which NF-kB-dependent transcriptional priming precedes NLRP3 inflammasome assembly and caspase-1 activation; diacerein is approved for osteoarthritis and is converted in vivo to rhein, an NF-kB inhibitor with inflammasome-attenuating properties, but its effect on the ischemic myocardial transcriptome was unknown. The design is a proper pretreatment-and-challenge model: male Wistar rats received oral diacerein at 80 mg/kg/day or vehicle for 10 days, after which hearts were isolated and subjected to global no-flow ischemia followed by reperfusion in a Langendorff preparation, with RNA sequencing of left-ventricular tissue. The result is a quantitatively specified transcriptional program - 314 differentially expressed genes, 268 downregulated and 46 upregulated - with downregulated genes including Selp (the endothelial adhesion molecule mediating leukocyte capture), Csf2rb (the shared GM-CSF/IL-3/IL-5 receptor subunit) and Ripk1, while Card9 was upregulated; gene set enrichment analysis showed suppression of TNF signaling, NF-kB signaling and cytokine-cytokine receptor interaction pathways. Why it matters for BB: the deliverable is a repurposing candidate with an approved parent drug, a defined active metabolite, a quantified dose, a defined pretreatment window, and a mechanistic narrative that places the drug upstream of inflammasome assembly rather than merely downstream of inflammation - the 'sterile inflammation priming' framing is directly transferable to BB's ischemia-reperfusion and inflammatory-priming briefs, and Csf2rb is a receptor-subunit node that can be read out as a pharmacodynamic panel rather than a static transcript list. Cautions: n=6 across two arms in rat left ventricle is a discovery-scale design; Langendorff ex vivo perfusion is not a whole-animal infarct model; and diacerein's osteoarthritis tolerability profile may not transfer to a cardioprotection indication.
