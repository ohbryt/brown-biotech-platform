### Post 7: Spatial Transcriptomics — Tumor Microenvironment Atlas 3개 종양 통합 종합

🗺️ **[Spatial Transcriptomics] SCLC 600K+ cells lymph node metastasis spatial ecosystem 분석(Zhang Cell Reports Medicine 2026) + gastric cancer lymphocyte-aggregated region(LAR) 세포 거동·면역 활성화 상관관계 종합(Gao Nature Communications 2026) + pan-cancer 56 LCPs 13 niches 종양 미세환경 통합 분석(Li Cell Reports Medicine 2026): Tumor Microenvironment Atlas 통합 종합(Synthesizing) 동시 확인**

Spatial transcriptomics 기반 **종양 미세환경 아틀라스 통합(Synthesizing)**이 출처에서 동시에 확인된다[11][12][13].

**SCLC Spatial Ecosystem: 600K+ Cells Lymph Node Metastasis Atlas[11]**
Zicheng Zhang et al.(Cell Reports Medicine 2026)의 연구는 SCLC의 lymph node metastasis(LNM) spatial cellular ecosystem을 분석한다. **CosMx Spatial Molecular Imager**: 75 patients, 105 primary/metastatic lymph node specimens. **600K+ cells**: comprehensive atlas. **LNM-enriched malignant subclusters 3개**: distinct metabolic·angiogenic programs. **Immune exclusion features**: spatially correlated with LNM. **Vascular-immune crosstalk**: endothelial cells가 malignant cells 회피하며 cytotoxic T cells와 functional perivascular niches 형성. **Pan-immune hotspot PIHs-1**: 독립적 survival predictor. **Spatial architectures**: mechanistic insights + translatable biomarkers. 8/24 Compiling에서 Compiling된 SCLC atlas가 오늘 Synthesizing에서 LNM 메커니즘 중심으로 종합.

**Gastric Cancer Lymphocyte-Aggregated Region(LAR): Spatial Immunology 종합[12]**
Sen Gao et al.(Nature Communications 2026)의 연구는 gastric cancer에서 lymphocyte-aggregated region(LAR)을 포함한 4개 spatial regions를 통합 분석한다. **LAR + tertiary lymphoid structures**: lymphocyte aggregates. **Naive T cell abundance**: T cell activation-associated pathways와 상관관계. **Lymphocyte distribution patterns**: 2개 transcriptomically distinct groups — Group A(activated lymphocytes adjacent to cancerous regions), Group B(resting lymphocytes). **Group A spatial proximity**: PD1+CD27+ CD8 T cells가 CD70+LAMP3+ dendritic cells와 근접. **Immunotherapy biomarkers**: LAR 기반 biomarker potential 확인. 핵심: gastric cancer spatial heterogeneity로 immunotherapy response 예측 biomarkers 제시.

**Pan-Cancer TSME: 56 LCPs + 13 Niches 통합 Atlas[13]**
Jiarong Li et al.(Cell Reports Medicine 2026)의 연구는 12 cancer types 373 samples에서 pan-cancer spatial transcriptomics를 수행한다. **56 local cellular programs(LCPs) + 13 recurrent niches**: systematic dissection. **Ligand-receptor analysis**: niche-shared + niche-specific interactions. **Cellular location dependence**: tumor cells + macrophages의 gene expression이 specific location에 의존. **Niche-clinical outcomes 상관관계**: Niche_4(macrophages + tumor cells) Poor prognosis + immunotherapy resistance. **Niche_11**(macrophages + immune cells) → better survival + treatment response. **Translational potential**: immunotherapy biomarkers + therapeutic targets. 핵심: pan-cancer TSME atlas로 cellular communication + spatial organization의 구조적 영향 종합.

**8월 24일 Compiling Day vs 오늘의 차별점**: 8/24 Compiling에서 **Zhang + Gao + Li**로 **TMEA(Tumor Microenvironment Atlas) 체계 Compiling**했다면, 오늘은 같은 데이터(각 2026년 paper)를 **LNM mechanism(Zhang) + LAR spatial immunology(Gao) + Niche outcome prediction(Li)**라는 **새로운 해석 각도**로 종합한다. Compiling(8/24) → atlas 체계 확립 → Synthesizing(8/26) → mechanism + immunology + clinical outcome prediction으로 통합 해석 종합.
