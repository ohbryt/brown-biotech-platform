📋 **[FDA/규제] CAR-T·TIL convergence paradigm 재확인, lifileucel(TIL, melanoma) · afamitresgene(TCR, synovial sarcoma) FDA 승인 2개 확인 + 3단계 barrier framework(Access·Recognition·Persistence) 체계화(Bach Cancer Cell International 2026) + FDA CGT 38개 through 2025 전수 분석, 73.4% postmarketing requirement + 17.9% primary efficacy endpoint 문제 재確認(Shahzad Clinical Pharmacology Therapeutics 2026) + FDA gene therapy approvals의 platform-aligned risk-based framework 구체화(Oo Journal Clinical Pharmacology 2026): FDA/규제의 Diversifying(다양화) 동시 확인**

FDA/규제의**다양화(Diversifying)**가 출처에서 동시에 확인된다[13][14][15].

**CAR-T·TIL Convergence Paradigm: 3-Stage Barrier Framework[13]**
Duc-Hiep Bach et al.(Cancer Cell International 2026)의 review는 CAR-T와 TIL therapy의 convergence paradigm을 분석한다. **2024년 milestone**: lifileucel FDA 승인 — 고형종양 최초 세포 치료 승인. **CAR-T vs TIL**: hematologic malignancies에서는 CAR-T 성공, solid tumors에서는 도전. **3-stage barrier framework**: (i) Access — stromal · vascular barriers로 T-cell tumor infiltration 제한, (ii) Recognition — antigen heterogeneity · immune evasion으로 malignant cells 식별 어려움, (iii) Persistence — immunosuppressive TME 내에서 T-cell function 유지 어려움. **Convergence paradigm**: CAR-T와 TIL이 동일한 biology barriers에 대해 complementary engineering · biological strategies로 대응. **Biomarker-guided patient selection**: tumor의 dominant biological barriers 기반으로 therapeutic selection. 핵심: CAR-T·TIL convergence + 3-stage barrier framework 체계화 — FDA 규제 Diversifying.

**FDA CGT: 38개 Through 2025 Postmarketing Evidence 문제[14]**
Mahnum Shahzad et al.(Clinical Pharmacology & Therapeutics 2026): FDA CGT 38개 through December 31, 2025 전수 분석. **Postmarketing requirements**: 73.4%가 ≥1 postmarketing requirement/commitment — 총 52 studies. **문제**: 17.9%만 primary clinical efficacy endpoint를 가진 postmarketing study 보유. **대부분 ongoing**: 현재 진행 중인 studies. 핵심: FDA CGT의 postmarketing requirement 문제 재확인 — FDA 규제 Diversifying.

**FDA Gene Therapy Approvals: Platform-Aligned Risk-Based Framework[15]**
C. Oo et al.(Journal of Clinical Pharmacology 2026): FDA gene therapy approvals의 진화와 future directions 분석. **최근 approvals 가속**: RNA-based agents, viral/non-viral in vivo platforms, ex vivo genetically modified cell therapies. **FDA initiatives**: plausible mechanism framework, CMC flexibility initiative, advanced manufacturing technologies program. **AI/ML-enabled analytics**: dose selection, safety evaluation, durability prediction 지원. 핵심: FDA gene therapy의 platform-aligned risk-based framework 구체화 — FDA 규제 Diversifying.
