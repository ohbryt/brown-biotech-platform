### Post 7: Spatial Transcriptomics/Cancer Atlas — Pan-Cancer Niche_4/11 Clinical Outcomes와 Spatial Omics의 실체적 근거

🗺️ **[Spatial Transcriptomics/Cancer Atlas] Li et al.으로 12개 암종 373 samples 56 LCPs + 13 niches + Niche_4 poor prognosis/immunotherapy resistance + Niche_11 better survival (Cell Reports Medicine 2026) + Zhang et al.으로 SCLC 75 patients 105 specimens 600,000+ cells + PIHs-1 survival predictor + vascular-immune crosstalk (Cell Reports Medicine 2026) + Cen et al.으로 single-cell/spatial omics methods 2025+ reviews surveyed — subcellular/high-throughput/multiomics profiling + generative AI (MedComm 2026): Spatial Transcriptomics Materializing(실체화) 동시 확인**

Spatial Transcriptomics/Cancer Atlas의**임상 결과 실체화와 방법론 표준화(Materializing)**가 출처에서 동시에 확인된다[11][12][7].

**Pan-Cancer Spatial Transcriptomics: Niche_4/11 Clinical Outcomes[11]**

Niche_4는 macrophages와 tumor cells colocalization으로 poor prognosis와 immunotherapy resistance를, Niche_11은 macrophages와 immune cells colocalization으로 better survival과 treatment response를 각각 예측한다. Spatial Materializing Consolidating.

**SCLC Spatial Transcriptomics: PIHs-1 + Vascular-Immune Crosstalk[12]**
Z. Zhang et al.(Cell Reports Medicine 2026)의 연구는 75 SCLC patients, 105 specimens, 600,000+ cells(CosMx Spatial Molecular Imager)에서 spatial transcriptomics를 수행한다[12]. 3개 LNM-enriched malignant subclusters가 distinct metabolic과 angiogenic programs와 immune exclusion features로 同定되고, PIHs-1(pan-immune hotspot 1)은 독립적 survival predictor로 확인된다. Vascular-immune crosstalk에서 endothelial cells가 cytotoxic T cells와 functional perivascular niches를 형성한다. Spatial Materializing Consolidating.

Biomarker discovery, therapeutic target identification, precision medicine에 활용되며, rapid methodological advances와 systematic application gap이 challenges로 제시된다. Spatial Materializing Consolidating.
