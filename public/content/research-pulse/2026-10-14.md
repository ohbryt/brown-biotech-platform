### Post 7: Spatial Transcriptomics — Gastric Cancer LAR + SCLC CosMx + Pan-Cancer Benchmarking

🗺️ **[Spatial Transcriptomics] Gao et al.로 gastric cancer LAR: Lymphocyte Aggregated Region + PD1+CD27+CD8 T cells near CD70+LAMP3+ DCs + tertiary lymphoid structures(Nature Communications 2026) + Zhang et al.로 SCLC CosMx: 600K+ cells + PIHs-1 survival predictor + LNM-enriched malignant subclusters 3개 + vascular-immune crosstalk(Cell Reports Medicine 2026) + Li et al.로 pan-cancer spatial: 12개 암종 373개 검체 + 56 LCPs + 13 niches + Niche_4 poor prognosis + Niche_11 better survival(Cell Reports Medicine 2026): spatial transcriptomics Benchmarking(평가) 동시 확인**

Spatial transcriptomics의 **gastric cancer LAR + SCLC CosMx + pan-cancer niche benchmarking(Benchmarking)**이 출처에서 동시에 확인된다[14][15][16].

**Gastric Cancer Lymphocyte Aggregated Region: PD1+CD8 near CD70+LAMP3+ DCs[14]**
Sen Gao et al.(Nature Communications 2026)의 연구는 gastric cancer의 Lymphocyte Aggregated Region(LAR)을 분석한다[14]. **4개 공간 영역 식별**: tumor microenvironment의 공간적 구조. **Lymphocyte Aggregated Region(LAR)**: tertiary lymphoid structures(TLS) 포함. **PD1+CD27+CD8 T cells**: CD70+LAMP3+ dendritic cells와 근접. **Group A vs Group B differential**: 더 활성화된 lymphocytes가 cancerous regions 근처, 더 resting lymphocytes가 Group B의 cancerous regions 근처. **면역 활성화 공간적 패턴**: immune activation의 공간적 조직. **면역치료 biomarker potential**: PD1+CD27+CD8 near CD70+LAMP3+ 패턴. 핵심: gastric cancer LAR — 공간적 면역 biomarker benchmark(Benchmarking).

**SCLC CosMx 600K+ Cells: PIHs-1 + Vascular-Immune Crosstalk[15]**
Zi-Cheng Zhang et al.(Cell Reports Medicine 2026)의 연구를 재확인한다[15]. **75명 105개 검체 600K+ cells**: SCLC spatial atlas. **3개 LNM-enriched malignant subclusters**: 림프절 전이 강화 종양 하위 집합. **PIHs-1**: 독립적 survival predictor. **Vascular-immune crosstalk**: endothelial cells가 immune activation orchestrate + cytotoxic T cells와 functional perivascular niches. 핵심: SCLC CosMx — spatial survival predictor benchmark(Benchmarking).

**Pan-Cancer Spatial Niche: Niche_4 vs Niche_11 Survival Differential[16]**
Jiarong Li et al.(Cell Reports Medicine 2026)의 연구를 재확인한다[16]. **12개 암종 373개 검체**: pan-cancer spatial analysis. **56개 LCPs + 13개 niches**: 공간적 세포 프로그램. **Niche_4(macrophage + tumor cell)**: poor prognosis + immunotherapy resistance. **Niche_11(macrophage + immune cell)**: better survival + treatment response. 핵심: pan-cancer niche — survival differential benchmark(Benchmarking).

**Gastric Cancer LAR vs SCLC vs Pan-Cancer Differential**: Gastric cancer LAR(Gao)은 **TLs + PD1+CD27+CD8 near CD70+LAMP3+ DCs**의 공간적 면역 패턴을, SCLC CosMx(Zhang)은 **LNM-enriched subclusters + PIHs-1**의 예후 예측자를, Pan-cancer niche(Li)는 **Niche_4/11 differential**의 보편적 패턴을 제시한다. 동일 공간 면역 영역이지만 gastric-specific immune activation pattern vs SCLC-specific LNM predictor vs pan-cancer universal survival niche로 차별화된다.

**10월 13일 Standardizing(Zhang spatial ecotypes + Nguyen TME + Li niche) vs 오늘의 차별점**: 10/13이 **Nguyen Clinical & Translational Immunology**으로 AI/ML cross-platform integration + standardized workflows를, **Zhang Nature**으로 spatial ecotypes를, **Li Cell Reports Medicine**으로 Niche_4/11를 제시했다면, 오늘은 **Gao Nature Communications**으로 **gastric cancer LAR — PD1+CD27+CD8 near CD70+LAMP3+ DCs + tertiary lymphoid structures**라는 **새로운 암종 적용(gastric cancer)에서의 공간적 면역 biomarker benchmark**를(Benchmarking)를, **Zhang 재확인**으로 **SCLC CosMx 600K+ cells + PIHs-1 + LNM-enriched subclusters**라는 **폐소세포암종 특이적 survival predictor benchmark**를(Benchmarking)를, **Li 재확인**으로 **pan-cancer Niche_4/11 survival differential**을(보편적 biomarker benchmark Benchmarking)를 동시에 제시한다. Standardizing(10/13) → gastric cancer LAR spatial biomarker + SCLC PIHs-1 predictor + pan-cancer Niche_4/11 benchmark Benchmarking(10/14).
